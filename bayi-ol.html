Frequently Asked Questions

What is a pleated fly screen and how does it work?

Pleated insect screen is a type of insect screen that can be easily closed when not in use thanks to its foldable structure and when opened, it prevents flies and other pests from entering. It is extremely easy to use and is mounted on window or door frames.

What are pleated curtains?

Pleated curtains are curtain types that can be opened and closed by folding and offer a modern, stylish look. They offer effective solutions for light control and privacy.
