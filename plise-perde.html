Pleated Curtain Systems

Changing living standards in recent years have allowed people to spend more time at home. In this context, people make a number of attempts to beautify their living spaces and lead a more comfortable life. One of the most important ones is Pleated Curtain (Glass Balcony Curtain). Although it is called Glass Balcony Curtain, its usage area is much more. It has two important features. The first is that it cuts the sun rays in unwanted situations, and the other important feature is that it covers the image in unwanted situations. It adds a different atmosphere to the spaces with different colors and different fabric options. AK Pleating Company is one of the leading companies in the sector with its rich color and fabric options. All systems and components are its own patented products.
