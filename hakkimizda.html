About Us

AkPlise maintains our leading position in the sector in the production and sales of pleated mosquito nets and pleated curtain materials in our office in Sancaktepe, Istanbul. In addition to high quality materials, our company provides a comprehensive service by offering all the necessary machines and apparatus to our customers during the production process.

Our comprehensive product range has been carefully selected and developed to provide the most suitable solutions for our customers' needs. Whether for homes or commercial spaces, our pleated mosquito nets and curtains offer a combination of aesthetics and functionality, making your living spaces more comfortable.

Quality

Our products and equipment are manufactured in accordance with high quality standards.

Technology

We serve with state-of-the-art production machines.

Full Support

We provide all the machines and apparatus you need in the production process.

Our Mission

As AkPlise, our mission is to increase the comfort and aesthetics of living spaces by providing our customers with high quality pleated mosquito nets and pleated curtain materials. With our advanced technology production facility and expert team, we strive to provide the fastest and most effective solutions to our customers' demands, to apply the highest quality standards in the sector and to keep customer satisfaction at the highest level.

Our Vision

AkPlise's vision is to become a nationally and internationally recognized and preferred brand in the pleated fly screen and pleated curtain sector. We aim to strengthen our leading position in our sector with our continuous innovative approaches, customer-oriented service approach and high quality standards. With our environmentally friendly production methods and technological investments, we aim to add value to living spaces by offering sustainable and long-lasting solutions.
With exports to our global markets, we are proud to deliver our quality products to our customers around the world. If you are looking for a reliable supplier worldwide, contact us.

Features of our Production Area

Spacious and Modern Facilities

Our 5000 m² production area is equipped with large capacity production lines and modern equipment. In this way, we are able to produce in high quantities and respond quickly to the demands of our customers.

Advanced Technology

We use the latest and most advanced technologies in our production processes. With plastic injection molding machines, folding machines and plastic extruder machines, we produce in accordance with high precision and quality standards.

Professional Team

Our team of expert engineers and experienced employees meticulously manage the production processes. By performing quality control at every stage, we ensure that our products comply with high standards.

Environmentally Friendly Practices

We attach great importance to environmentally friendly practices in our production facility. We minimize our environmental impact by using energy efficient systems.

Safety and Occupational Health

The safety and health of our employees is one of our top priorities. We work in accordance with the highest safety standards in our production area and strictly comply with occupational health rules.

Production Capacity

Our production area in Tuzla, Istanbul offers fast and effective solutions to all demands of our customers with its high production capacity. With our wide product range in pleated mosquito nets and pleated curtain materials, we are able to produce for all kinds of needs.

5000 m² Professional Production Area in Tuzla, Istanbul

With our quality, innovation and customer satisfaction-oriented service approach, we operate in our 5000 m² fully professional production area in Tuzla, Istanbul. Our modern production facility, equipped with the latest technologies, enables us to be the leader in the production of pleated mosquito nets and pleated curtain materials.
